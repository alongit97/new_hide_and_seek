We are asking you to participate in a research study. This form will give you some information about the study. Please read this form carefully.

A. Project Description
The game involves a set of {{ num_boxes }} boxes and a number of items to be hidden in them. Each box has a value by which the number of items in that box will be multiplied (the box’ multiplication rate). You will play either as a “Hider” or as an “Opener”. If you are a Hider, you will allocate the items endowed to you across the boxes; if you are an Opener, you will choose {{ boxes_to_open }} of the boxes to be opened later. The Opener earns the value of the items that are found in the opened {% if boxes_to_open > 1 %}boxes{% else %}box{% endif %}, the Hider earns the value of the items in the remaining, unopened {% if num_boxes == 3 and boxes_to_open == 2 %}box{% else %}boxes{% endif %}.
Prior to the game you will be shown a simple test of 3 questions to ensure you understood the game. You will get two chances to answer each question correctly. If you answer any question twice incorrectly, your participation will be terminated. After playing, you will be asked to answer a question about the game.

This study is part of a research being conducted with the goal of publication in academic journals and presentation at academic conferences.

B. Risks and Benefits

  1. Your participation in this study does not involve any physical or emotional risk to you beyond the risks of daily life.
  2. You have the right to discontinue participation at any time for any reason; but if you do, you will forfeit your payoff.
  3. The potential benefit of the study is a better scientific understanding of people’s decision making.

C. Confidentiality
We do not have access to your identity, nor do we have any need to know it. Hence, your privacy will be maintained in all published and written data resulting from this study. All data collected will be analyzed in an aggregate form.

D. Contacts
If you have any questions or concerns about this study or rights as a participant in this research, you may contact yaakov.kareev@mail.huji.ac.il

E. Subject Rights
Participation in this study is voluntary.
